Our 8-Step Free Credit Research Process

Step 1: Identify the Real Search Intent

We begin by determining what the reader is trying to accomplish rather than treating every keyword containing "free credit" as the same topic.

Examples include:

  • free kredit RM3 — amount-based intent;
  • link free kredit RM3 — link-discovery plus claim intent;
  • link free kredit RM10 — link-discovery plus RM10 intent;
  • free slot RM100 — higher-value promotional amount intent;
  • freekredit88 — RM88-related promotional intent.

Step 2: Identify Who Is Responsible for the Claim

We identify whether the information originates from:

  • a gaming platform;
  • a game provider;
  • a campaign;
  • a link or discovery service;
  • an individual game;
  • an independent third-party website; or
  • historical promotional material.

This prevents unrelated entities from being merged into one claim.

Step 3: Classify the Promotion

We determine what kind of offer is actually being discussed.

A promotion may be:

  • no-deposit;
  • new-registration based;
  • deposit related;
  • provider specific;
  • game specific;
  • campaign based;
  • cashback or rebate based; or
  • subject to another qualifying action.

Methodology rule: the promotional amount and the qualification method are separate facts.

Therefore: "RM10 free credit" does not automatically mean "RM10 no-deposit free credit."

Step 4: Check Eligibility and Registration Conditions

We look for conditions relevant to searches such as register free kredit , register dapat free credit, new register free RM3 and claim free credit RM3.

Depending on the promotion, we may check:

  • whether the user must be new;
  • whether one promotion is allowed per person;
  • whether phone verification is required;
  • whether account verification is required;
  • whether a qualifying action must be completed;
  • whether geographic restrictions apply; and
  • whether minimum-age requirements apply.

Registration itself is not treated as proof of bonus eligibility unless the applicable promotion establishes that relationship.

Step 5: Check Wagering, Game and Expiry Conditions

Promotional value can depend heavily on conditions that appear after the headline.

Where available, we evaluate:

  • wagering or turnover requirements;
  • games that count toward wagering;
  • excluded game categories;
  • maximum bet conditions;
  • promotion expiry;
  • bonus-balance expiry; and
  • other conditions that materially affect usage.

These checks are especially relevant when researching a free kredit slot promotion or free kredit slot for new users.

Readers can also review our free kredit turnover requirements guide.

Step 6: Check Withdrawal Conditions

We do not assume that promotional credit is equivalent to immediately withdrawable money.

Where disclosed, we check:

  • turnover required before withdrawal;
  • maximum withdrawal from a promotion;
  • verification requirements;
  • minimum withdrawal thresholds;
  • promotion-specific withdrawal restrictions; and
  • whether promotional balance itself can be withdrawn.

For more context, see: can you withdraw free kredit?

Step 7: Compare the Claim With the Available Evidence

We compare the wording used in search results, promotional pages and supporting information.

Where the evidence does not establish a claim, our preferred approach is to:

  • qualify the statement;
  • state what is known;
  • state what still requires verification; or
  • avoid making the claim entirely.

Step 8: Review Information When It Changes

Promotional information can become outdated more quickly than evergreen educational information.

We may update an article when:

  • a promotion expires;
  • eligibility changes;
  • wagering requirements change;
  • withdrawal limits change;
  • provider availability changes;
  • a platform changes its information;
  • a material factual error is identified; or
  • new evidence materially changes our explanation.

Research Methodology Questions

How does FreeKreditRM10 determine whether an offer is no-deposit?

We look for current information establishing that a qualifying deposit is not required to receive the promotion. The promotional amount alone does not establish no-deposit status.

Does free kredit RM10 automatically mean RM10 can be withdrawn?

No. Free kredit RM10 generally describes promotional account value. Withdrawal can depend on wagering, verification and promotion-specific conditions.

How do you verify registration bonuses?

We check whether registration is sufficient or whether additional eligibility, verification, campaign or qualifying-action requirements apply.

How do you research JILI free kredit?

We separate JILI as a game-provider entity from the platform responsible for the promotion, then determine whether JILI games are actually included under the applicable promotional rules.

Do search rankings prove that a bonus exists?

No. Search-query and ranking information can show what readers search for, but it is not evidence that a particular promotion currently exists.

How are old promotions handled?

Historical information can be preserved when useful, but it should be clearly distinguished from current promotional information.
