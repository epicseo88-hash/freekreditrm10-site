Our Editorial Principles

FreeKreditRM10 content is guided by six core principles.

  1. Accuracy before promotion. We aim to explain what can reasonably be established rather than repeating the most attractive promotional headline.
  2. Separate facts from claims. A platform's promotional statement is identified as a promotional claim unless independently established as a broader fact.
  3. Separate entities. Platforms, game providers, directories, individual games and promotions are not automatically treated as the same entity.
  4. Explain important conditions. Where relevant, our content considers eligibility, deposits, wagering, expiry, game restrictions and withdrawal limitations.
  5. Avoid guarantees. We do not treat a promotional amount, game outcome or withdrawal as guaranteed simply because it appears in a headline or advertisement.
  6. Update when information changes. Time-sensitive promotional information may be revised, qualified or removed when newer information becomes available.

What Sources Do We Prefer?

The reliability of a statement depends partly on the source supporting it. Where possible, we prefer information closest to the entity responsible for the claim.

These may include official platform pages, official promotion terms, official game-provider information, policies and other first-party documentation.

Secondary Sources

Secondary sources may be used for additional context, discovery or comparison when primary information is unavailable or when multiple perspectives are useful.

Historical Sources

Historical material can help explain an older indexed page or previous promotion, but it should not automatically be presented as evidence of a current offer.
