What Does Free Kredit Mean?

Free kredit generally means promotional account credit issued under a specific campaign. It is not automatically cash, does not automatically mean no deposit, does not guarantee eligibility or winnings, and may be subject to turnover, game restrictions, expiry, maximum cash-out and withdrawal conditions.

Free Kredit

Definition: Promotional account credit issued under campaign-specific conditions.

Free kredit should not automatically be treated as cash. A promotion can define who qualifies, whether a deposit is required, which games are eligible, what turnover applies, when the credit expires and how much may eventually become eligible for withdrawal.

Free kredit ≠ cash.

Turnover and Wagering Glossary

Turnover

The amount of qualifying wagering that may need to be completed before an eligible promotional balance can proceed toward withdrawal.

Applicable Wagering Base × Turnover Multiplier = Nominal Turnover Target

Read: Free Kredit Turnover Requirements.

Turnover Multiplier

The number used to calculate the nominal wagering target.

For example, if an educational example uses RM10 promotional credit and 10× turnover:

RM10 × 10 = RM100 nominal turnover target

This is an example only, not a claim about a current promotion.

Game Contribution

The percentage of wagering on a particular eligible game that counts toward turnover.

Example Contribution RM10 of Play Counts As
100% RM10 qualifying turnover
50% RM5 qualifying turnover
0% RM0 qualifying turnover

Percentages above are educational examples only.

Maximum Bet

A promotional limit on how much can be wagered per spin, round or transaction while a bonus remains active.

Available balance ≠ permitted promotional bet size.

Wagering Requirement

Another term commonly used for turnover or playthrough conditions.

Free Kredit Glossary: Common Questions

What does free kredit mean?

Free kredit generally means promotional account credit issued according to a campaign's conditions. It should not automatically be treated as cash.

What does free kredit RM10 mean?

It means RM10 in promotional account credit under a specific offer. It does not automatically mean RM10 can immediately be withdrawn.

What does free kredit tanpa deposit mean?

It describes promotional credit issued without requiring a qualifying deposit beforehand. Other conditions such as eligibility, turnover and expiry may still apply.

What is turnover?

Turnover is qualifying wagering that may need to be completed before an eligible promotional result can proceed toward withdrawal.

What is game contribution?

Game contribution is the percentage of wagering on an eligible game that counts toward a turnover requirement.

What is maximum cash-out?

Responsible Gaming Terminology

Free kredit should not be treated as income, guaranteed profit or a way to recover previous losses.

Provider names, promotional amounts, jackpots, no-deposit language and registration bonuses do not change the fact that gambling outcomes are uncertain.

Readers should:

  • participate only where legally permitted;
  • meet applicable age requirements;
  • use only discretionary funds they can afford to lose;
  • set time and spending limits;
  • avoid borrowing money to gamble;
  • avoid chasing losses;
  • avoid increasing bets simply to complete turnover;
  • avoid claims promising guaranteed winnings or jackpots; and
  • stop if gambling begins to affect finances, work, relationships or wellbeing.
