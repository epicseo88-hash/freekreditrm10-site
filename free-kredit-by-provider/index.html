What Does Free Kredit by Provider Mean?

A provider-focused keyword combines two different types of information:

Provider: the company supplying the game or game catalogue.

Free Kredit: the promotional credit offered under a campaign.

For example, JILI free kredit normally signals that a user wants to know whether promotional credit can be used with JILI games.

It does not automatically mean:

  • JILI itself issued the promotion;
  • every JILI game qualifies;
  • all participating platforms offer the same promotion;
  • the free-credit amount is fixed;

  • the same turnover applies everywhere; or
