11. Check Maximum Cash-Out and Minimum Withdrawal

Promotional amount, displayed balance, maximum cash-out and minimum withdrawal are separate values.

12. Check Verification and Withdrawal Rules

Confirm what account verification and withdrawal-method requirements apply after promotional conditions are completed.
