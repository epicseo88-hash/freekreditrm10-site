Free Kredit Terms & Conditions Red Flags

Be cautious when a promotional page makes a large claim but does not clearly explain the conditions behind it.

Potential warning signs include:

  • “guaranteed withdrawal” without clear supporting terms;
  • “no conditions” claims that conflict with published rules;
  • no identifiable promotion period;
  • no explanation of eligibility;
  • no explanation of turnover where wagering applies;
  • no explanation of withdrawal restrictions;
  • unclear platform identity;
  • old screenshots presented as proof of current promotions;
  • unexpected requests for money solely to unlock supposed free credit; or
  • requests for passwords or one-time passwords from an unrelated third party.

A promotional headline should never replace the current terms and conditions.
