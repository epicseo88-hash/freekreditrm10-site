What Is Joker Gaming RTP?

There is no single Joker Gaming RTP that applies to every game. RTP, or Return to Player, is a theoretical long-term percentage associated with a particular game and configuration. Different Joker slots can have different RTP settings, so players should check the information for the exact title and version they are playing.

What Does RTP Mean?

RTP stands for Return to Player.

It describes a theoretical long-term return built into a game model over a very large number of rounds.

For example, if a game displays a theoretical RTP of 96%, that does not mean:

  • RM100 played returns exactly RM96;
  • the next session will return 96%;
  • a player has a 96% chance of winning;
  • the next spin is predictable; or
  • losses must soon be recovered.

Why We Do Not Publish One Joker Gaming RTP

What Does Joker Slot Volatility Mean?

Volatility describes the general distribution of a slot game's outcomes. Lower-volatility games are generally designed around more frequent smaller outcomes, while higher-volatility games can concentrate more of their return into less frequent larger outcomes. Volatility does not predict what the next spin will do.

Understanding Joker Slot Volatility
Volatility General Mathematical Profile What It Does Not Mean
Lower Returns may generally be distributed through more frequent smaller outcomes. It does not guarantee a longer session or profit.
Medium Balance between frequency and size can sit between lower and higher volatility profiles. It does not mean outcomes are predictable.
Higher More of the mathematical return may be concentrated into less frequent larger outcomes. It does not mean a large win is due.
