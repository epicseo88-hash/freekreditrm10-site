What Is the RTP of JILI Games?

There is no single RTP percentage for all JILI games. RTP is a game-level and potentially configuration-specific characteristic, so players should check the exact JILI title and version available through the platform rather than relying on a generic “JILI RTP” number.

What Does RTP Mean?

RTP means Return to Player.

It describes a theoretical long-term statistical characteristic of a game.

It does not predict what one player will receive from:

  • one spin;
  • ten spins;
  • one session;
  • RM10;
  • RM100; or
  • any other short sample.

Example of RTP Interpretation

Suppose a particular game version had a theoretical RTP of 96%.

That would not mean:

  • RM100 must return RM96 during your session;
  • RM10 must return RM9.60;
  • a losing session is due to recover;
  • the next spin has a 96% chance of winning; or
  • a particular player will experience the theoretical percentage.
