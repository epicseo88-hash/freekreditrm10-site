What Does RTP Mean?

RTP means Return to Player.

It describes the theoretical long-term return of a game over a very large statistical sample.

Even if an Animal Band implementation displayed 96% RTP, that would not mean:

  • RM100 returns RM96 during your session;
  • 10 rounds must return 96%;
  • a losing streak guarantees a future win;
