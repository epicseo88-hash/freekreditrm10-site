What Does RTP Actually Mean?

RTP means Return to Player.

It describes a theoretical long-term return across a very large statistical sample.

It does not promise what one player will receive during one session.

For example, a theoretical RTP near 94% does not mean:

  • RM100 must return approximately RM94 during your session;
  • a losing RM50 means the next RM50 must recover the difference;
  • the machine keeps a personal balance owed to you;
