</p>

Types of JILI Bonuses You May Encounter
</h2>

</h3>

confirm that no deposit is required.
</p>

</h3>

</p>

</h3>

a deposit match or another campaign mechanic.
</p>

</h3>

<p>
A reward calculated from qualifying activity rather than supplied
simply for registering.
</p>


<h3>

</h3>

<p>
Promotional credit restricted to eligible JILI slot games or a
slot category that includes JILI.
</p>
