  <p>

  </p>

      </tr>

      <tr>
        <td>Promotional Credit</td>
        <td>The promotional value credited to an eligible account.</td>
      </tr>

      <tr>
        <td>Turnover / Wagering</td>
        <td>The required amount of qualifying play defined by the promotion.</td>
      </tr>

      <tr>
        <td>Wagering Multiplier</td>
        <td>The multiplier used in the turnover calculation, such as 5× or 10×.</td>
      </tr>

      <tr>
        <td>Wagering Base</td>
        <td>The amount to which the multiplier applies, such as bonus only or deposit plus bonus.</td>
      </tr>

      <tr>
        <td>Eligible Contribution</td>
        <td>The portion of a qualifying bet that counts toward turnover.</td>
      </tr>

      <tr>

<section id="calculation">

  <h2>How to Calculate Free Kredit Turnover</h2>

  <p>
    For a promotion using a straightforward multiplier, the basic formula is:
  </p>

  <div class="formula-box">
    <strong>Turnover Target = Wagering Base × Turnover Multiplier</strong>
  </div>

  <p>
    The critical phrase is <strong>wagering base</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    Different promotions may calculate turnover using:
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li>promotional credit only;</li>
    <li>deposit only;</li>
    <li>deposit plus promotional credit;</li>
    <li>eligible winnings;</li>
    <li>or another amount specifically defined in the terms.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Example A: Bonus-Only Calculation</h3>

  <div class="example-box">
    <p>Promotional credit: <strong>RM10</strong></p>
    <p>Turnover: <strong>10× bonus</strong></p>
    <p>Calculation: <strong>RM10 × 10 = RM100</strong></p>
    <p>Illustrative turnover target: <strong>RM100</strong></p>
  </div>

  <h3>Example B: Deposit Plus Bonus Calculation</h3>

  <div class="example-box">
    <p>Deposit: <strong>RM10</strong></p>
    <p>Promotional credit: <strong>RM10</strong></p>
    <p>Turnover: <strong>10× deposit + bonus</strong></p>
    <p>Calculation: <strong>(RM10 + RM10) × 10 = RM200</strong></p>
    <p>Illustrative turnover target: <strong>RM200</strong></p>
  </div>

  <p>
    Both examples use a 10× multiplier, yet the required turnover is different because the calculation base is different.
  </p>

  <div class="warning-box">
    <p>
      <strong>Always read what the “×” applies to.</strong>
      A 10× turnover requirement does not have one universal meaning.
    </p>
