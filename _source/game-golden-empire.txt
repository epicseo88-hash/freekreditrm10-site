<section id="gameplay">

<h2>
How Does Golden Empire Slot Work?
</h2>

<p>
The <strong>Golden Empire game</strong> uses a dynamic slot
structure rather than relying only on a static set of reels and
ordinary fixed-line wins.
</p>

<p>
The core gameplay cycle can be understood as:
</p>

<ol>

<li>Set the permitted stake.</li>

<li>Spin the reels.</li>

<li>Matching combinations are evaluated.</li>

<li>Winning symbols can be eliminated.</li>

<li>The reel surface can change as symbols are removed.</li>

<li>New symbols enter the available positions.</li>

<li>Additional combinations may be evaluated.</li>

<li>Golden Frame positions can create Wilds.</li>

<li>Scatters can trigger Free Games.</li>

<li>Multipliers can accumulate during Free Games.</li>

</ol>


<h3>
Why Golden Empire Feels Different From a Basic Slot
</h3>

<p>
Three mechanics interact with each other:
</p>

<ul>

<li><strong>dynamic reels;</strong></li>

<li><strong>symbol elimination;</strong></li>

<li><strong>Golden Frame Wild transformations.</strong></li>

</ul>

<p>
This means understanding the game requires more than simply reading
the value printed beside each symbol in the paytable.
</p>

</section>


<!-- =========================================================
MEGAWAYS
========================================================= -->

<section id="megaways">

<h2>
What Does 32,400 Megaways Mean in Golden Empire?
</h2>

<p>
JILI lists Golden Empire with a maximum of
<strong>32,400 Megaways</strong>.
</p>

<p>
In practical terms, the game uses a dynamic reel structure where
the number of possible symbol combinations can change as the reel
surface changes.
</p>

<p>
Therefore, it is more useful to think of Golden Empire as a
<strong>ways-to-win style dynamic slot</strong> than as a traditional
slot with only a small fixed number of paylines.
</p>


<h3>
Does 32,400 Ways Mean 32,400 Wins?
</h3>

<p>
<strong>No.</strong>
</p>

<p>
The number describes the maximum potential ways in which qualifying
symbol combinations can be evaluated under the game's structure.
It does not mean a spin creates 32,400 winning combinations.
</p>


<h3>
Does More Ways Mean a Guaranteed Better Result?
</h3>

<p>
No.
</p>

<p>
The number of ways is part of the game's mathematical structure.
It does not predict the result of the next spin.
</p>

</section>


<!-- =========================================================
CASCADE
========================================================= -->

<section id="cascade">

<h2>
How Do Golden Empire Cascades and Eliminations Work?
</h2>

<p>
JILI describes Golden Empire as using a dynamic reel where winning
symbols can be eliminated, allowing the reel surface to transform.
</p>

<p>
The simplified sequence is:
</p>

<p>
<strong>
Spin → Winning Combination → Symbol Elimination → Reel Change →
New Symbols → Additional Evaluation
</strong>
</p>

<p>
This process is commonly described as a cascade or tumble-style
mechanic.
</p>


<h3>
Why Does Symbol Size Matter?
</h3>

<p>
JILI specifically notes that symbol size is relevant in Golden
Empire.
</p>

<p>
Larger symbols can survive multiple eliminations, with JILI
describing the possibility of up to four eliminations before the
symbol disappears completely.
</p>

<p>
This adds another layer to the cascading mechanic because not every
displayed symbol necessarily disappears after a single qualifying
elimination.
</p>

</section>


<!-- =========================================================
GOLDEN FRAME
========================================================= -->

<section id="golden-frame">

<h2>
What Is the Golden Frame Feature?
</h2>

<p>
The <strong>Golden Frame</strong> is one of the defining mechanics
of Golden Empire.
</p>

<p>
According to JILI's game rules, Golden Frames appear on the
<strong>second, third, fourth and fifth reels</strong>.
</p>

<p>
Symbols appearing within qualifying Golden Frame positions can be
transformed into <strong>Wild symbols</strong>.
</p>


<h3>
Why Is the Golden Frame Important?
</h3>

<p>
Wild transformation can affect the formation of subsequent winning
combinations because Wild symbols can substitute for other eligible
symbols according to the game rules.
</p>

<p>
This connects the Golden Frame directly with the game's cascading
structure:
</p>

<p>
<strong>
Golden Frame → Wild → Potential Combination → Elimination →
Reel Transformation
</strong>
</p>

</section>

<h2>
How Does the Wild Symbol Work in Golden Empire?
</h2>

<p>
JILI's current rules state that Wild symbols are associated with
the middle reel area — reels two through five.
</p>

<p>
Wilds can substitute for eligible regular symbols to help form
winning combinations according to the paytable.
</p>


<h3>
Does a Wild Guarantee a Win?
</h3>

<p>
No.
</p>

<p>
A Wild is a game mechanic rather than a guaranteed payout.
Its value depends on:
</p>

<ul>

<li>where it appears;</li>

<li>the surrounding symbols;</li>

<li>the current reel configuration;</li>

<li>the qualifying ways; and</li>

<li>the applicable game rules.</li>

</ul>

</section>

</h2>

Free Game features.
</p>

<p>
JILI states that during Free Games, symbol eliminations can cause
the multiplier to accumulate.
</p>

<p>
JILI describes this multiplier as having
<strong>no stated upper limit within the feature description</strong>.
</p>


<h3>
What Does an Unlimited Multiplier Mean?
</h3>

<p>
It means the feature description does not specify a fixed multiplier
ceiling in the way that some games might cap a multiplier at a
particular value.
</p>

<p>
It does <strong>not</strong> mean:
</p>

<ul>

<li>the multiplier will increase indefinitely in every session;</li>

<li>a high multiplier is guaranteed;</li>

<li>the maximum game win is unlimited; or</li>

<li>future eliminations can be predicted.</li>

</ul>

<p>
Golden Empire still has a provider-listed maximum win of
<strong>2,000×</strong>.
</p>
