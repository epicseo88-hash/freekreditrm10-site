    <h2>Our Editorial Principles</h2>

    <p>
      FreeKreditRM10 content is guided by six core principles.

    <ol>

        than repeating the most attractive promotional headline.

        qualified or removed when newer information becomes
        available.

        a separate promotion condition. The two should not be
        treated as interchangeable.
      </p>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Does no deposit mean there are no wagering requirements?</summary>
      <p>
        No. A promotion can require no qualifying deposit while
        still applying wagering, verification, game, expiry or
        withdrawal conditions.
      </p>
    </details>

      <summary>Does registering guarantee free credit?</summary>
      <p>
        No. Registration and promotion eligibility are separate.
        New-member, verification and other requirements may apply.
      </p>
    </details>

    <details>
      <summary>Does JILI issue every JILI free credit promotion?</summary>
      <p>
        Not necessarily. JILI is a game provider. A platform may
        run a promotion involving JILI games, but the provider and
        promotion issuer should be identified separately.
      </p>
    </details>
