<section id="red-flags">

  <h2>12 Free Kredit Scam & Red Flag Warning Signs</h2>

  <div class="checklist-box">

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 1: Guaranteed Winnings</p>
      <p>
        Claims such as “guaranteed win,” “100% guaranteed profit” or “cannot lose” should not be treated as reliable promotional information.
      </p>
      <p>
        Gambling outcomes are uncertain. Promotional credit does not remove that uncertainty.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 2: Guaranteed Withdrawal</p>
      <p>
        A promotion should not be interpreted as guaranteed withdrawal simply because promotional credit has been issued.
      </p>
      <p>
        Turnover, verification, minimum withdrawal, maximum cash-out and other current rules may apply.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 3: Requests for Your Password or OTP</p>
      <p>
        Do not provide your account password, banking password or one-time password to someone claiming it is necessary to activate free credit.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 4: Unclear Website Identity</p>
      <p>
        If you cannot determine which platform operates the website or promotion, verify it before registering or providing information.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 5: No Eligibility Information</p>
      <p>
        “Everyone gets RM10” or similar wording deserves additional verification when the actual eligibility conditions are missing.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 6: Misleading No-Deposit Wording</p>
      <p>
        A promotion described as no deposit should not secretly require a qualifying deposit before the promotional credit is issued.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 7: Hidden Turnover</p>
      <p>
        If promotional credit is subject to turnover but the requirement is difficult to locate or understand, do not evaluate the promotion by headline amount alone.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 8: Missing Eligible-Game Rules</p>
      <p>
        Do not assume every game displayed on a platform contributes toward a promotion.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 9: No Expiry Information</p>
      <p>
        Promotions can have claim deadlines, credit expiry and turnover deadlines. Missing timing information makes an offer harder to evaluate.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 10: Maximum Cash-Out Is Hidden</p>
      <p>
        A large promotional balance can still have a separate cash-out limit. Verify it before assuming the displayed balance can be withdrawn.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 11: An Old Promotion Is Presented as Current</p>
      <p>
        Search results, screenshots and old articles can remain online after a promotion has changed or ended.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="red-flag">
      <p class="red-flag-number">Red Flag 12: Pressure to Act Immediately</p>
      <p>
        Be cautious when someone pressures you to deposit, transfer money, reveal credentials or make a decision before you can verify the current terms.
      </p>
    </div>

  </div>

  <h2>Responsible Gaming Is Part of Promotion Safety</h2>

  <p>
    A promotion can be genuine and still be unsuitable for a particular user.
  </p>

  <p>
    Safety therefore includes checking your own spending and time limits rather than focusing only on whether a promotion exists.
  </p>

  <div class="warning-box">
    <p>
      <strong>
        Do not borrow money, chase losses, increase your spending limit or continue playing simply because promotional credit is available or approaching expiry.
      </strong>
    </p>
  </div>

  <p>Ask yourself:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>Am I legally permitted and eligible by age to participate?</li>
    <li>Do I understand the promotion before participating?</li>
    <li>Can I afford any personal money involved?</li>
    <li>Have I set a spending limit?</li>
    <li>Have I set a time limit?</li>
    <li>Would I still participate without the promotion?</li>
    <li>Am I comfortable allowing the promotion to expire instead of chasing turnover?</li>
  </ul>
