          <th scope="col">Example</th>

      </thead>

      <tbody>

        <tr>
          <td>Correction</td>
          <td>Previously published information was materially inaccurate.</td>
          <td>A page states RM10 when the verified offer was RM5.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr>
          <td>Update</td>
          <td>The information was accurate but later changed.</td>
          <td>A promotion previously offered RM10 but later changed to RM5.</td>

        <tr>
          <td>Clarification</td>
          <td>The underlying information may be correct but needs clearer explanation.</td>
          <td>Explaining that promotional credit is not automatically withdrawable cash.</td>
        </tr>

        <tr>
          <td>Editorial Improvement</td>
          <td>The information remains accurate but presentation or usability is improved.</td>
          <td>Adding a comparison table or clearer internal navigation.</td>
        </tr>

      </tbody>

    </table>

    <h2>Our 6-Step Correction Process</h2>

    <h3>1. Identify the Disputed Information</h3>

    <p>
      We first identify the exact claim, page, table, link or statement that may be inaccurate.
    </p>


    <h3>2. Review the Original Context</h3>

    <p>
      We examine how the information is presented and whether the surrounding context changes its meaning.
    </p>


    <h3>3. Check Current Sources</h3>

    <p>
      Where possible, we prioritise primary or authoritative sources relevant to the disputed claim.
    </p>

    <p>
      Depending on the issue, this may include official platform pages, official promotion terms, provider information, campaign pages or other directly relevant documentation.
    </p>


    <h3>4. Determine Whether a Change Is Required</h3>

    <p>
      We compare the published claim with the available evidence and determine whether the page requires a correction, update, clarification or no change.
    </p>


    <h3>5. Correct the Content</h3>

    <p>
      When the evidence supports a correction, we update the affected statement and surrounding information where necessary so that the page remains understandable.
    </p>


    <h3>6. Check Related Pages</h3>

    <p>
      If the same claim may appear elsewhere, we may review related FreeKreditRM10 pages to reduce the risk of inconsistent information across the website.
    </p>
