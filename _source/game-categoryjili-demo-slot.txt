<section id="rtp">


<h2>
    What Does RTP Mean in a JILI Demo Slot?
</h2>


<p>
    RTP stands for <strong>Return to Player</strong>.
</p>


<p>
    It is a theoretical long-run percentage used to describe how much
    of total wagered value a game is designed to return across a very
    large number of plays under defined conditions.
</p>


<p>
    RTP is often misunderstood.
</p>


<h3>
    RTP Does Not Mean:
</h3>


<ul>

    <li>
        you will receive that percentage back during one session;
    </li>

    <li>
        every player receives the same result;
    </li>

    <li>
        a game must return money after a specific number of spins;
    </li>

    <li>
        today's losses guarantee tomorrow's wins; or
    </li>

    <li>
        demo results predict the next real-money session.
    </li>

</ul>


<p>
    A short demo session is therefore not a reliable way to calculate
    the long-run behaviour of a slot.
</p>


</section>

<section id="volatility">


<h2>
    What Is Volatility in JILI Games?
</h2>


<p>
    <strong>Volatility</strong> describes the general distribution
    pattern of outcomes rather than whether a particular game is
    “good” or “bad.”
</p>


<p>
    In simplified terms:
</p>


<ul>

    <li>
        <strong>lower volatility</strong> generally describes a design
        with comparatively smaller and potentially more frequent changes
        in balance;
    </li>

    <li>
        <strong>medium volatility</strong> sits between the extremes; and
    </li>

    <li>
        <strong>higher volatility</strong> generally describes a design
        where results can be more uneven and larger swings may occur.
    </li>

</ul>


<p>
    Volatility is particularly useful to examine in demo mode because
    it helps users understand a game's risk profile without assuming
    that a few demo spins establish a guaranteed pattern.
</p>


</section>

</h2>


<p>
    JILI titles do not all use the same mechanics.
</p>


<p>
    Different games can use different paylines, reel layouts and
    special features.
</p>



<h3>
    Paylines
</h3>


<p>
    A payline defines one possible path used to create a qualifying
    symbol combination in games that use fixed-line mechanics.
</p>



<h3>
    Wild Symbols
</h3>


<p>
    Wilds commonly interact with normal symbols, but their exact
    behaviour should be checked in the individual game rules.
</p>



<h3>
    Scatter Symbols
</h3>


<p>
    Scatters may activate a free-game or bonus feature when the required
    conditions are met.
</p>



<h3>
    Free Spins
</h3>


<p>
    Free spins are a game mechanic and should not be confused with
    promotional <strong>free credit</strong>.
</p>


<p>
    A free-spin feature occurs inside a game, while free credit generally
    refers to promotional account credit.
</p>



<h3>
    Multipliers
</h3>


<p>
    Multipliers can increase the value of a qualifying result when
    the conditions defined by the game are met.
</p>



<h3>
    Respins
</h3>


<p>
    Some games may provide additional spins or respins as part of
    a feature sequence.
</p>
