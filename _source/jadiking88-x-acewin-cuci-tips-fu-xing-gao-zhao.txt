<section id="243-ways">

<h2>
What Does 243 Ways to Win Mean?
</h2>

<p>
A <strong>243-ways-to-win</strong> system differs from a traditional
fixed-payline slot.
</p>

<p>
Instead of requiring matching symbols to sit on one specific
payline, qualifying combinations are generally formed through
matching symbols appearing on consecutive reels according to the
game's pay rules.
</p>

<p>
The exact symbol requirements should always be confirmed from the
game's paytable.
</p>

<h3>
Does 243 Ways Mean 243 Wins Every Spin?
</h3>

<p>
<strong>No.</strong>
</p>

<p>
It describes the game's potential combination structure.
It does not mean every spin contains 243 winning combinations.
</p>

<h3>
Does More Ways to Win Mean Guaranteed Profit?
</h3>

<p>
<strong>No.</strong>
</p>

<p>
Ways-to-win structure is only one component of the game's
mathematical model.
</p>

<p>
RTP, symbol distribution, feature probability, payout values and
volatility also matter.
</p>

</section>
