<section id="rtp">

<h2>
What Is RTP in Pragmatic Play Games?
</h2>

<p>
<strong>RTP</strong> means <strong>Return to Player</strong>.
</p>

<p>
It is a theoretical percentage calculated over a very large number
of game rounds.
</p>

<p>
For example, if a game has a theoretical RTP of 96%, this does
<strong>not</strong> mean a player who spends RM100 will receive RM96 back.
</p>

<p>
Individual sessions can be far above or below the theoretical percentage.
</p>

<h3>
Why RTP Must Be Checked by Game
</h3>

<p>
Pragmatic Play's own game documentation demonstrates that RTP differs
between individual titles.
</p>

<p>
Official game information has historically shown examples around
96% or above, but each game's current information screen should be
treated as the relevant source for that title.
</p>

<p>
Some games may also have different RTP configurations or different
figures depending on optional gameplay modes.
</p>

<h3>
What RTP Does Not Tell You
</h3>

<ul>

<li>
It does not predict the next spin.
</li>

<li>
It does not guarantee a return during one session.
</li>

<li>
It does not tell you how frequently wins occur.
</li>

<li>
It does not replace volatility information.
</li>

<li>
It does not make a slot profitable or predictable.
</li>

</ul>

</section>
